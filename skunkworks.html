PROTOTYPE

BLOB

working title
v0.1 · WINDOWS

Two-player chain-reaction on a hex board. Tap a tile to grow your blob; push it past max stage and it cascades into the neighbours. Whoever owns the last blob standing wins. Play against another person or the computer.

GENRETactical chain-reaction, hex-grid, turn-based
PLATFORMWindows x64, self-contained
ENGINEGodot
STATUSPlayable prototype

tap a tile to grow. max stage = cascade into neighbours.

two seats: human + human, or human + AI (pluggable).

per-blob seeded wobble / ripple: no two look identical.

right-drag = orbit, scroll = zoom. wood-grain board.
