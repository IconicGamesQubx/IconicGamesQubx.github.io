PSYCHOGORILLA
— working title —
v0.1 · BUILD OK

Single-room stealth. You share a prison cell with a gorilla. Finish your chores before the timer runs out — quietly enough that the gorilla doesn't snap. The core tension: get work done vs. don't wake the thing in the corner.

GENREStealth, single-room, real-time
PLATFORMWindows x64, self-contained
ENGINEGodot
STATUSOne-build prototype — may or may not return

movement makes noise. so does work.

noisy tasks: 4s. silent tasks: 10s. pick your poison.

gorilla state machine: sleeping → pacing → fixated → enraged

once enraged, contact = caught

PROTOTYPE
BLOB
— working title —
v0.1 · BUILD OK

Two-player chain-reaction on a hex board. Tap a tile to grow your blob; push it past max stage and it cascades into the neighbours. Whoever owns the last blob standing wins. Pluggable AI on the other seat.

GENRETactical chain-reaction, hex-grid, turn-based
PLATFORMWindows x64, self-contained
ENGINEGodot
STATUSOne-build prototype — feel-focused

tap a tile to grow. max stage = cascade into neighbours.

two seats: human + human, or human + AI (pluggable).

per-blob seeded wobble / ripple — no two look identical.

right-drag = orbit, scroll = zoom. wood-grain board.
