Can I adjust the experience?

Choose your difficulty, remap controls, and adjust visual effects with reduced motion, reduced flashes, and high contrast shapes.

The interface supports English, French, German, Spanish, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, and Welsh.

Can I stream the game and its music?

Yes. You can stream, record, and monetize content featuring Qubx 2 and its soundtrack. Our music has no royalty obligations or DMCA claims.

The Qubx 2 soundtrack is by the Qubx2 Dev Team. Generative AI tools were used in parts of its creation, as disclosed on Steam.
