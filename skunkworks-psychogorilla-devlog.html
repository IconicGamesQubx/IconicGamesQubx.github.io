    v0.1
    One cell, one gorilla, five chores. 180-second timer. Heat builds from movement and from noisy tasks; silent tasks are slower but safer. The cellmate escalates through four states — sleeping, pacing, fixated, enraged — and at enraged, contact ends the run. — The four-state escalation is the part that's working: you can feel the room get tighter without a single number on screen. Loss copy is still placeholder. May or may not get a v0.2 — that's what skunkworks is for.
