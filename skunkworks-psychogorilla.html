  1. 2026-05-02
    v0.1
    One cell, one gorilla, five chores.
    New
    • 180-second timer; five chores to finish before the buzzer.
    • Heat builds from movement and from noisy tasks; silent tasks are slower but safer.
    • Cellmate escalates through four states: sleeping, pacing, fixated, enraged. Contact while enraged ends the run.
    The four-state escalation is the part that's working: you can feel the room get tighter without a single number on screen. Loss copy is still placeholder. May or may not get a v0.2: that's what skunkworks is for.
