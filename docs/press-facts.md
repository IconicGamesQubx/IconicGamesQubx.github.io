# Qubx 2

A reflex action puzzler by Iconic Games.

Rotate a cube in three dimensions to match the shapes coming toward it.
Keep a combo going as the pace rises. Run mode adds chapters, the Qubit
Shop, and powerups; Quick Play offers an endless score challenge.

## At a glance

- Developer and publisher: Iconic Games
- Release: January 31, 2026, in Early Access
- Steam platforms: Windows, macOS, Linux, Steam Deck
- Controls: keyboard and controller
- Players: single-player
- Steam price: US$2.99, checked September 9, 2026. Regional prices vary.
- Demo: free on the Steam store page
- Interface languages: English, French, German, Spanish (Spain), Japanese,
  Simplified Chinese, Welsh
- Accessibility: difficulty settings, control remapping, reduced motion,
  reduced flashes, and high contrast shapes

Early Access work focuses on difficulty, the run economy, and game feel.
There is no fixed date for leaving Early Access. See Steam for the latest
product information, system requirements, and price.

## Music and streaming

Qubx 2's soundtrack is by the Qubx2 Dev Team. Generative AI tools were used
in parts of its creation, as disclosed on the Steam store page.

You can stream, record, and monetize content featuring Qubx 2 and its
soundtrack. There are no royalty obligations or DMCA claims on the game's
music. This policy applies to Qubx 2. Qubx Classic's soundtrack is credited
separately to DrGonzo_404.
